Bundle sjl-12-001, part C's controls K1 and K2: the four parse-corpus reports of the patched builds, named and
not retained. Written 2026-10-26, at the records step (WP7.3), from the scratch copies the after steps wrote.

The rule retains each control "with their output" (part C's controls clause). What decides each control is
retained in this folder:
  K1  k1.patch; k1-checks.log.gz and k1-checks-named-rows.txt (the source-order row fails);
      k1-identity-base.tsv.gz, part B's comparison of base run 1 against the K1 report (byte differences reported),
      and k1-identity-after.tsv.gz, the same against the after run 1, which isolates the patch; k1-revert-check.txt.
  K2  k2.patch; k2-checks.log.gz and k2-checks-named-rows.txt (the determinism row fails);
      k2-determinism.tsv.gz, the four-hash comparison over runs 1 to 3 (it lists every compilation whose four
      results are not one accepted hash, with all four results); logs/k2-parse-corpus-run3.stdout.txt (run 3's own
      count of compilations whose two concurrent compilations differ); k2-revert-check.txt.
  Both  k-reverted-checks.log.gz and k-reverted-checks-named-rows.txt (the reverted copy, rebuilt, passes every row).
  Each run's standard output, standard error and exit are in logs/.

the bundle keeps its growth near the base's. Each comparison output above names its report by the path it was to
have in this folder, because the comparisons ran with the reports copied there and removed afterwards
